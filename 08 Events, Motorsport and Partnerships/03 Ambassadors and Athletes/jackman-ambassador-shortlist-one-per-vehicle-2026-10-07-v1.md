## Three things worth deciding before outreach starts

**1. Name a "platform of record" in each contract.**
Several of these people own more than one truck — @groovyrunner.gx has a 4Runner and a GX550, @14smilespergallon has three Fords, @395rig has a Power Wagon and a Silverado. That is efficient, but it also means two ambassadors could end up posting the same platform. Write the specific vehicle into each agreement so the roster stays genuinely one-per-vehicle.

**2. Tacoma needs two slots, not one.**
It is the dominant platform in this category and you have four strong candidates — two of whom you are already talking to. Splitting by **generation** is the clean answer: 3rd gen and 4th gen are different fitments and different buyers, so @trdpro_pugz and @ghostpro.la are not duplicates. That also keeps @22protaco and @campygilmore available as the warm backups they already are.

**3. The private code decision solves the dealer problem.**
Worth recording: a code that is handed out person-to-person and never posted publicly does not undercut dealer pricing where dealers can see it. That resolves the channel conflict I flagged in the founder brief, and it should be written into the contract as an obligation — *the code is not to be posted publicly* — with a stated consequence if it leaks. Codes do leak onto coupon aggregator sites; review redemption volume monthly against each ambassador's audience size.
