# Jackman Wheels Ambassador Program

### Founder brief · 7 October 2026 · Austin Fitzgerald

**Purpose of this document:** to get three decisions made so the program can launch on 1 January 2027.
Full operating detail sits in the program design document; this is the short version.

---

## The problem I am trying to solve

We are asking people to spend real money on a wheel they have never seen on a truck like theirs.

- **We have almost no proof the product exists in the wild.** No library of real installs, on real trucks, shot properly. Every brand we compete with has hundreds of these.
- **Our own audience is small.** The Instagram account started this quarter at 760 followers. Paid social can buy reach, but it cannot buy credibility, and cold traffic to an unproven brand converts badly.
- **Credibility in this category is borrowed, not bought.** Buyers decide by looking at builds they admire. We need to be on those builds.

An ambassador program solves all three at once, and it is the cheapest way to do it.

---

## What I am proposing

**Sign eight ambassadors to a one-year sponsorship for 2027**, reviewed in October 2027 to decide on 2028.

Five mechanics:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Term** | Signature through 31 December 2027. One shared renewal date |
| **Content** | One post per month, in their own voice. We never write the caption |
| **Events** | Display their vehicle at Southern California shows — one ambassador per show |
| **Announcements** | Up to six times a year they push a launch or an offer to their audience |
| **Compensation** | Tiered: some receive a set of wheels, some a deep discount. Each gets a discount code to hand out |

The year-long term is deliberate. Monthly one-off posts are forgettable; a year of someone genuinely running our wheels is a track record.

## Three decisions I need from this meeting

**1. The discount codes versus the dealer channel.**
This is the one that genuinely needs founder sign-off. We have 25 dealers in progress, and their reason to stock us is margin. If eight ambassadors hand out codes that undercut dealer pricing, we are competing with the people we are asking to carry inventory — and they find out, because customers show them the code.

My recommendation: **keep the friend code at 10–15%**, which sits below typical dealer margin, so a dealer can still compete on price and service. The longer-term answer is making codes redeemable at dealers too, which turns ambassadors into dealer traffic instead of dealer competition.

**2. Free wheels, or discount only?**
My recommendation: free sets for the four Founding ambassadors, deep discount for the rest. The four receiving sets are the ones delivering photography and event presence — things we would otherwise purchase.

**3. The budget ceiling.**
Without a number, "we will review each one individually" has no limit. A ceiling turns every individual negotiation into a straightforward yes or no.

## What we get back, and how we will judge it

Tracked per ambassador, every month: code redemptions and revenue, posts delivered against the twelve committed, events attended, and content assets received.

**One expectation I want to set now, before anyone asks in March.**

Direct revenue through discount codes will probably not cover the cost of the wheels in year one. That is normal for a first-year program, and it is not what we are buying.

**Year one buys proof.** A library of real installs we own and can use in every ad, email and dealer conversation. A visible roster that makes the brand look established. Eight credible people whose audiences have now seen our wheels on a truck they respect.

**Judge 2027 on assets and credibility. Judge 2028 on revenue.**

## The honest risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Dealer resentment over codes | Decision 1 above. Settle it before any code is issued |
| An ambassador goes quiet | Monthly minimum written in, 90-day checkpoint, 30-day termination either way |
| Reputational exposure from tying the brand to an individual | Conduct clause in every agreement |
| No fitment for an ambassador's truck | Audit the eight vehicles against stock **before** anyone is signed |
| Capacity — one person running eight relationships | Start at eight. Expand mid-2027 only if the rhythm is holding |
