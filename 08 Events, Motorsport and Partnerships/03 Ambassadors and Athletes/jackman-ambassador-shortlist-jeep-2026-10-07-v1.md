## Why Jeep was thin, and why that was wrong

Worth being straight about this. Jeep did not get missed by accident — I down-weighted it on purpose.

Your original brief named Toyota, Ford, Chevy, GMC and Ram, so when I built the scoring model I gave Jeep-only accounts a **zero on platform gap**. That pushed genuinely strong accounts like @trailrecon and @thestorytillnow down the rankings for a reason that no longer applies now the founders want vehicle diversity.

It was also the wrong commercial call. **Jeep is the largest aftermarket wheel market in the United States** — Wrangler owners modify at a higher rate than any other platform, and they change wheels more often than they change almost anything else. On a pure revenue-per-ambassador basis Jeep probably deserves more than one slot.

---

## Jeep is not one platform

The same logic that applies to Tacoma generations applies harder here. These are different fitments, different bolt patterns in some cases, and genuinely different buyers:

| Sub-platform | What it is | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrangler JL** | 2018 onward | Newest money, most likely to buy a full wheel-and-tire package |
| **Wrangler JK** | 2007–2018 | The modification sweet spot. Cheap enough to cut up, old enough to need wheels |
| **Gladiator JT** | 2020 onward | Truck buyers who wanted a Jeep. Overlaps your overland positioning |
| **Rubicon 392** | V8 halo | Small, loud, aspirational |
| **TJ / XJ / classic** | Pre-2007 | Restoration and budget-build scene |

Treat JL, JK and JT as three separate roster slots, not one "Jeep" slot.

## The event lead, which may be worth more than any single ambassador

**@jeepxjdanny** has 220 followers, so he is not an ambassador. But he is a **board member of the San Diego Jeep Club** and a member of **Tierra Del Sol**.

Tierra Del Sol runs the **TDS Desert Safari** in Ocotillo Wells — one of the largest Jeep events in the country, held annually in Southern California, typically in March. That is a local, Jeep-specific, high-density event with a club structure behind it, and it lines up exactly with the founders' push for SoCal event presence.

Second event route: **@grdloc** runs Great American Crawl. An ambassador who brings an event is worth more than one who attends yours.
