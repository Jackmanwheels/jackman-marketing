# Brand Change Proposal — Establishing 1968 as the Founding Date

**Proposed by:** Austin Fitzgerald · **Date:** 2026-09-15 · **Status:** awaiting ratification
**Classification:** **MAJOR** — a standing prohibition is being lifted, and copy currently in market becomes wrong.
**Decision source:** `00 Marketing Command Center/06 Standards and Conventions/jackman-decisions-inbox.md` → `D-01`

---

## Why this is a proposal and not an edit

Same reason as the founder-names proposal: governance is explicit that there is **one
canonical guide and nobody forks it.** Guardrail #5 and the `jackman-brand-guidelines`
skill are source files. Changes get proposed, the brand owner edits the source, bumps
the version, and announces it.

Every change below is *current text → replacement text*, with a file and line number. No
skill file or guardrail has been edited.

---

## The change

**The founding date is resolved. Jackman Wheels was established in 1968.**

Approved forms: **"Established 1968"** (long) and **"Est. 1968"** (short). The positioning
line this unlocks is **"Established 1968. Rebirthed 2026."**

This lifts the prohibition in guardrail #5, which currently reads *"No founding decade or
year. The founding date is unresolved. Use 'California-born.'"*

**What this is worth.** Lineage is the only position in this category that a competitor
cannot buy their way into. Raceline's closest equivalent is "family owned since 1996" and
they under-tell it. Vossen states a founding year in their bio without hesitating; we
haven't been able to. Every one of the four Hoonigan brands is selling performance.
Being 28 years older than the nearest heritage claim in the category is the whole
argument, and until today we were prohibited from making it.

## Replacement vocabulary

Use these consistently so one decision doesn't produce nine phrasings.

| Retire | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "California-born" *(as a hedge for the missing year)* | "Established 1968" |
| "California-born since the 1960s" | "Established 1968" |
| "born in a California garage" | **unchanged — keep.** Still the best heritage sentence we have. Now pairs with the year rather than substituting for it. |
| *(no equivalent existed)* | "Established 1968. Rebirthed 2026." |
| "Est. 1968" as a standalone promo graphic | **retire the format, not the words.** See caveat below. |

**"California-born" is not retired as a phrase** — only as a *substitute for the year*.
It remains correct and useful; it just no longer has to do the work of a date.

## One caveat that survives the decision

**The "Est. 1968" promo graphic is still the worst-performing format we have.** The
strategy's Finding 2 measured it at 1,597 against an 11.9K best — and that pattern holds
across every brand benchmarked (Method's "SWEET DEALS" at 860× below their best, Vossen's
"NOW HIRING" at 175×).

Ratifying the date does not ratify the text-graphic format. **The words are approved. The
poster is not.** 1968 earns its keep inside real content — a period ad beside the modern
equivalent, an original catalogue page, a photograph of the garage — not as type on a
background.
