Austin is the photographer, designer and videographer for a department of one. One person producing about 4 posts a week plus daily Stories can't invent the brief each Monday. This skill walks him into a shoot with a shot list, not a mood (strategy `C-01`).

## 1. Gather the facts (ask only what's missing, in one message)
- **Date, location(s), and light window** (sunrise/sunset times for the day; run `date`, don't guess).
- **Vehicle(s) and wheels available** (make, model, year, which Jackman wheel/finish is on or going on). If a vehicle has no verified fitment in the lineup, say so and route the question to Clyde Wheeler; never state fitment yourself.
- **Time budget and crew** (alone? helper? drone? gimbal?).
- **Is a customer or ambassador involved?** If yes, the permission route applies (step 4).

## 2. Read the sources of truth (every run)
1. `00 Marketing Command Center\06 Standards and Conventions\jackman-decisions-inbox.md`: read first; check `Status:` before relying on any rule.
2. `03 Content and Social\jackman-social-strategy-2026-09-15-v2.md` (latest version): the six pillars, four jobs, and **Part 6, the current mix gap**. Plan shoots to close the gap (as of v2, Proof sits at 0% against a 15% target, and 32% of the grid hits no pillar).
3. `03 Content and Social\01 Content Calendar\jackman-content-formats-2026-10-06-v1.md`: the **ten named formats** and their shoot notes (for example Stock vs. Jackman needs the *before* captured first).
4. `03 Content and Social\jackman-social-playbook-2026-09-14-v1.md`: the never-ships list. Key rules that bite on shoots: no promo/sale graphics as feed posts (16), don't tag vehicle manufacturers (10), hashtags only for what's actually in frame (19), Collab in-grid, never download and re-upload (11), credit photographer and owner in the first two lines (13).
5. Existing captions and posts already drafted in `02 Social Posts and Captions`, so you don't re-plan shots that exist.
If a newer version of any file exists, use it and say which.

## 3. Build the plan
Output one document:
- **Goal line:** what this day is for, in plain words, and which pillar gap it closes.
- **Shot list**, grouped into blocks by **location, then light** (golden hour last or first; detail/studio shots in harsh midday; moving shots when the light is right). Each shot: number, **format name**, **one pillar**, **one job**, the exact frame (angle, subject, what's in it), photo/video/both, aspect ratio (4:5 feed, 9:16 Reels/Stories), and the vehicle visible in it. One pillar, one job per shot; if a shot would do two, force a choice.
- **Sendable check:** for each shot, one line on why a guy would send it to his buddy with the same truck. If you can't write that line, cut or rework the shot. Mark any shot that would grade as "None, decoration" and fix it with a vehicle, a before/after or a demotion to a carousel slide.
- **Capture-once list:** things that can't be re-shot (the stock "before", the unboxing, the first start-up, the owner's reaction). Put these first in the day.
- **Gear and prep checklist**, **shot-day run sheet** with times, and a **fallback** for weather or a late vehicle.
- **Output budget:** how many feed posts, Reels and Stories this day should yield, tied to the weekly cadence, so the batch actually covers the calendar.

Don't write captions. Hand finished concepts to `content-agent` for drafting and to `social-review` before anything is posted. If Austin wants a quick grade on the shot list now, run `social-review` on the riskiest two or three concepts.

## 4. Permissions, rights and disclosure
- Customer or ambassador vehicle: written permission, screenshotted and filed in `03 Content and Social\04 UGC and Permissions`. If there's a signed ambassador agreement, the content grant lives there; ask for **raw files**, not just exports.
- Free product or a code involved means FTC disclosure on their posts.
- Photographer credit and owner handle go in the first two lines of any post.

## 5. Deliver
Write the plan to `03 Content and Social\01 Content Calendar\jackman-shoot-day-plan-YYYY-MM-DD-v1.md`, then run `ship-doc` so Austin has a .docx and .pdf. Link both. Offer one line: "Want me to queue the captions with content-agent?" and nothing more.

## Rules
- Plans only. Never post, schedule or send. Never state specs, load ratings, torque values, part numbers or prices; route them (specs to Clyde Wheeler, dealer and MAP to Bud Jerue, claims and warranty to Kris Maya).
- Heritage content that depends on the founding date follows the decisions inbox (`D-01`); check its `Status:` and don't decide it yourself.
- Keep it realistic for one person. If the list needs more than the time budget, cut shots, don't compress the day.
