## STEP 5 — PLAIN-ENGLISH CALLOUTS

Every metric the founders might misread gets a short boxed callout titled WHAT THIS MEANS the FIRST time it appears (one or two sentences, 30 words or fewer, plain English, and say what it is NOT). Never repeat one; all of them also appear on the closing page 'How to read these numbers'. Definitions (do not contradict; if a figure you cite differs by platform, say so):
- Instagram views: each time a Reel plays or a post or Story appears on screen. Reels must play a moment and replays are not counted; for photos and Stories repeats are. It is NOT different people, that is reach. Example for a figure like 45K: 'Instagram counts a view every time a Reel plays or a post appears on screen, so 45K views is NOT 45,000 different people. Different people is reach.'
- Reach: different accounts that saw the content at least once.
- Engagement rate: likes, comments, saves and shares divided by reach.
- YouTube views: a viewer-started play that watches a meaningful stretch (commonly cited around 30 seconds; YouTube does not publish an exact rule).
- Sessions (Shopify): a visit to the store; ends after 30 minutes of inactivity, so one person can make several. NOT people.
- Visitors: different people (browsers) who visited; always lower than sessions.
- Conversion rate: sessions that ended in a completed checkout divided by sessions; 0.2% is about 2 purchases per 1,000 visits.
- Organic search sessions: visits from unpaid Google or Bing results.
- Search impressions: how many times the site appeared in Google results; it is NOT visits. A click is a visit. Click-through rate is clicks divided by impressions.
- Average position: where the site ranks on average in Google results (1 is the top; 8 to 10 is the bottom of page one; above 10 is page two or later).
- Branded vs generic search: branded means the searcher typed 'Jackman'; generic means they searched for wheels without knowing the name. Branded is demand we already have; generic is new customers.
- Direct traffic: visits with no recorded source (typed address, bookmarks, some apps and private shares).
- Followers vs email list: followers sit on rented platforms; the email list is an audience we own.
- Cost per session / per follower: spend divided by that result (not computable while spend or follower data is missing; say so).
- Growth score: our 0-100 blend; 100 means exactly on pace for 25% a month; the live score counts sessions pace and execution only (community and engagement are not scored).
- 25% month-over-month: each month 1.25x the last; about 14.5x over 12 months.
