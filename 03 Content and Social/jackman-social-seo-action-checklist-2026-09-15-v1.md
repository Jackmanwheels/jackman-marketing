Both conflicts are resolvable in a ten-minute call; neither should stall the 40-odd
items that don't touch them.

**1. The 1968 problem.** Kevin's central recommendation is to plant the flag on
*"Established 1968. Rebirthed 2026"* — he calls it the one thing competitors can't
copy, and he's right that lineage is the only defensible position we have. But the
strategy doc records three founding claims live in market right now (IG bio says 1968,
homepage says "California-born since the 1960s," Our Story puts the hand-built wheels
in the mid-1970s), and holds the line at **California-born, no year**, until the family
confirms it.

Both are correct. They're in sequence, not in conflict: **settle the date, then plant
the flag.** A heritage brand that gets its own founding year disproved in one click has
a worse problem than a vague one. Item **B-01** is the unblock, and it gates the
highest-value content pillar in the plan — treat it as the single most important line
on this list.

**2. Five pillars vs. six.** Kevin proposes Heritage / Customer builds / Made / Fitment
& tech / Brand & culture. The strategy v2 pillars (Branding, Technical·Proof,
Technical·Mechanism, Partnerships, Heritage, Creative) are **canonical** — they're wired
into the `social-review` skill and the desk card. Don't stand up a third naming system.
Kevin's set maps cleanly:

| Kevin's pillar | Canonical pillar |
|---|---|
| Customer builds 30% | Branding 35% + Partnerships 15% (Collab, not repost) |
| Fitment & tech 20% | Technical · Proof 15% + Technical · Mechanism 15% |
| Heritage 20% | Heritage 10% IG / 25% FB |
| Made / behind the scenes 20% | Technical · Mechanism, shop-floor cut |
| Brand & culture 10% | Creative 10% |

The one place Kevin is additive: his **ten repeatable formats** (§4) are format specs,
not pillars, and they slot under the canonical six without disturbing anything. Adopt
those wholesale.

## Standing rules — not tasks, standards

These don't get checked off. They're the bar every post clears, from Kevin's §6.

**Every post, without exception:** belongs to a pillar · front-loads keywords in the
first 125 characters · names year/make/model + wheel model + size + finish · has
human-written alt text · uses 3–5 specific hashtags including `#JackmanWheels` · ends
with one concrete ask · tags the owner, the shop and the adjacent brands · has burned-in
captions and on-screen text from frame one on video · gets a first-hour reply pass.

**Never:** post a watermark or anything that reads as recycled · post a bare product
render with no vehicle and no context · put stock music over machining or engine audio ·
buy followers or use engagement pods · leave a customer tag unacknowledged past 24 hours ·
go dark more than 3 days without Stories.

**The one line worth memorizing:** write every caption for the send, not the like.
Sends carry several times the distribution weight of a like. Saves rank next, then
comments, then likes.

## Targets — and which ones are actually the steering wheel

Kevin's 12-month follower target is **25,000–40,000** from 756. That's a 33–53x in a
year. Achievable for a relaunched heritage brand with product shipping, but it's an
*outcome*, not a lever — there's nothing you can do on Monday morning that moves it
directly.

The metrics below are the steering wheel. They're leading, they're controllable, and if
they're right the follower number takes care of itself.

| Metric | Target | Why it's the real one |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** | 1%+ | Top-weighted ranking signal. The single most important number here. |
| **Non-follower reach** | 60%+ of total | The only real source of new followers. |
| **Saves per post** | 2%+ of reach | Ranks second, and it's what fitment content earns. |
| **Net new followers/week** | 400–800 by month 3 | The outcome the three above produce. |
| **Mentions & tags/week** | trend up | Health of the UGC engine. |

Weekly, in a spreadsheet, Monday. **Look at the trend, not the week.**

Monthly, add: website sessions from social · organic search sessions · keyword rankings
for the fitment pages · dealer sign-ups. Those four are where social is supposed to show
up eventually — if they don't move in 90 days, something upstream is wrong.
