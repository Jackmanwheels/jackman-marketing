## Part 1 — The rules

These don't change week to week. Everything in Part 2 is subject to them.

### A. Never ships

1. **No founding decade or year.** The homepage says 1960s, Our Story says mid-1970s. Until that's settled: "California-born," "born in a California garage," "founded by Harry and Sheryl Jackman."
2. **No load rating, certification, torque value, or strength claim.** No JWL, VIA, SAE, or TÜV. None of it is published, and a revived brand cannot absorb one wheel-failure story.
3. **Never "lifetime warranty" unqualified, never any implication of coverage off-road.** Approved: "Limited lifetime structural warranty and a five-year finish warranty — see full terms," always linked.
4. **™ on exactly three names, first use only:** Radial Flow Form™ (R-FF™), Reverse Draft Technology™, Dual Vector Retention™ (DV-RET™). Never on Enhanced Bead Retention, Fixed Cap Orientation, or Water Relief Channels. Never verb or pluralize a mark — "Radial Flow Formed" is wrong.
5. **R-FF™ copy comes from `FF-Flowform-v2.html` and nowhere else.** The story is porosity closure and fragmented silicon. Never grain-fiber language, never a weight percentage, never "same as flow forming," never a comparison that disparages our own cast line.
6. **No rendered or comped wheels presented as photography.** Real installs only. Comps live in openly stylized graphic treatments or nowhere.
7. **Never grade a finish toward what we wish it looked like.** Finish accuracy is a returns issue.
8. **Classic 68 keeps its name.** Never "J68" in copy, whatever the filenames say.
9. **Fitment questions get "confirm with your dealer."** Never a spec from memory, in any comment, on any platform.
10. **Never tag a vehicle manufacturer.** It implies a relationship we don't have.

### B. Other people's content

11. **UGC in the grid is a Collab, or it goes in Stories.** Never download and re-upload. This is the whole answer to the April 2026 originality rule, and it's free.
12. **Written permission before anything, screenshotted, filed in `04 UGC and Permissions`.** A fire emoji is not a license.
13. **Credit the photographer and the owner by handle in the first two lines.**
14. **Never crop out a competitor's product to make a shot usable.** Either the shot works or it doesn't.

### C. Format and craft

15. **Every Reel starts as a Trial Reel.** Non-followers only, 72 hours. At 760 followers there is no reason to ever publish a Reel cold.
16. **No sale or promo graphics as feed posts.** Empirically the worst-performing format in the category — Method's promo reel pulled 4,536 views against a 3.9M best. Promotion goes in Stories, email, and dealer pages.
17. **Shoot for the slot.** 4:5 feed, 9:16 vertical. Never crop one into the other.
18. **First line is a claim, a number, or a question.** Never a greeting, never the brand name. "Fixed Cap Orientation means the logo sits level every time," not "Introducing the new J72!"
19. **5–10 hashtags.** Two brand, two or three category, two or three culture tags matching the vehicle actually in frame. Never tag a vehicle-culture hashtag for a rig that isn't in the photo.
20. **Bronze type never on a graphite panel** — 2.87:1, and it fails by looking fine on your monitor and vanishing on a phone in daylight. Warning text in bone white, never signal red on black.

### D. Rhythm

21. **One job per post** — reach, convert, de-risk, or hold. A post doing two does neither.
22. **Answer every comment within a few hours.** On Facebook this is not service, it's distribution — an active thread keeps a post circulating for days.
23. **Sustainable beats heroic.** Three weeks of five-a-day followed by silence is the fastest way to look like a brand in trouble.

### E. The paid trigger — a rule, not a date

24. **Boost nothing. Run winners.** Paid does not rescue unproven creative; it scales proven creative. A Trial Reel that performs with cold non-followers *is* your ad creative, already validated for free. Nothing goes behind spend until organic has identified it.
25. **The exception is Facebook.** FB will not grow from 15 followers organically — there is no seed audience to expand. When dealers sign or paid turns on, FB becomes real. Until then it gets built correctly and left alone.

## Part 4 — What we're measuring

Tied to what you're actually accountable for: awareness, clicks, conversion, and demonstrable partnerships.

| Metric | Where | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** | IG post insights | The signal that actually drives unconnected reach. Method's two breakouts were sendable; their pretty trucks weren't. |
| **% of reach that is unconnected** | IG insights | At 760 followers this is the only reach that exists. If it isn't the overwhelming majority, nothing is working. |
| **Trial Reel → live conversion** | Manual log | How many test Reels earned a full publish. This is your creative hit rate and it becomes your paid shortlist. |
| **Link clicks and landing sessions** | GA / Shopify | The awareness-to-site number you're measured on. |
| **Email captures from route downloads** | Klaviyo | The only audience Meta can't throttle. The real long-term asset. |
| **Collabs published per month** | Manual | Direct proxy for "showing good partnerships," and the fastest borrowed reach available. |
| **Dealer kit adoption** | Manual | How many dealers actually posted. Supports the sales team without owning their number. |
| **Not measured** | — | Follower count on its own. Likes. |

One filled-in monthly template in `10 Analytics and Insights` becomes a twelve-month record, and that record is the budget argument.

## Part 5 — Pre-flight, every post

1. Which of the four jobs is this doing? (One.)
2. Would someone send this to a friend?
3. UGC in the grid — Collab, and permission filed?
4. Any number, spec, rating or certification that isn't published? Then it comes out.
5. ™ correct — the three marks only?
6. Founding decade mentioned? Then it comes out.
7. "Lifetime warranty" unqualified, or any implication of off-road coverage? Then it comes out.
8. Bronze type on graphite?
9. Run `scripts/brand_check.py` on the caption.
