Off-Road Expo is in Costa Mesa this weekend, and Jackman Wheels will be there. Find us at **booth 9001-LI** at the OC Fair & Event Center, **Saturday and Sunday, October 3-4, 2026.**

This is our first show, and we're bringing the good stuff.

## What's at the booth

**Display vehicles.** The headliners are a H1 Hummer and a Meyers Manx, with more rigs on the way. Come see what our wheels look like on real builds, not render-world trucks.

**The full Street line.** Every wheel we make, in person, so you can pick one up and look at it from the back. That includes our forged Sprinter wheel and our forged beadlock.

**A wheel from the past.** We're also bringing an original Jackman wheel from back in the day. If you owned a set, ran a set, or remember a buddy who did, we want to hear about it. Bring the story.

## Win a set of wheels

We're giving away one set of wheels from the Street line. Your pick of the line, free to enter.

Signing up takes about a minute. Use the iPad at the booth, or enter from your phone: **[GIVEAWAY ENTRY URL]**. The winner is drawn **Monday, October 5, 2026.** One entry per person, 18+, US residents only. Read the full [official rules]([OFFICIAL RULES URL]).

## Come hang out

Honestly, the best part of a show like this is the conversation. Ask us anything about Jackman, the wheels, the old days, or what's coming next. Tell us what you're building. Argue with us about beadlocks. We'll be around both days and we'd like to meet you.

The off-road crowd is who we build for, so there's no better place to be this weekend.
