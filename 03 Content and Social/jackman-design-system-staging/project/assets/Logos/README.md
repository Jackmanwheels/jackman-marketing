Official Jackman marks. The artwork is pure black or pure white; it is never recolored.

- **jackman-lockup-stacked-black / -white**: primary lockup, JACKMAN over WHEELS, 2.38:1. `-black` art goes on light grounds, `-white` art on dark.
- **jackman-badge-j-black / -white**: circular J badge, square. Center caps, avatars, patches, and any slot too small for the lockup.
- **PDF** files are the vector masters for print, cutting and etching. **PNG** files are for screen.
- The artwork has no padding. Add 1X clear space (cap height of JACKMAN) on every side. Minimums: lockup 140px or 1.0in, badge 24px or 0.375in.
- Single-ink marks: the black files are ink #000000 and the white files are #FFFFFF. Show them on `surface` or a solid ground only.
