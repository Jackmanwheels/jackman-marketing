The title block opens every Word document and PDF. Provide a title, a document type, a date and a version.

- Light theme: `surface` bone (or white when printed on an office printer) with `ink` text.
- Top row: `jackman-lockup-stacked-black` at least 1.0in wide with 1X clear space, top-left. Document type, date and version to its right in `eyebrow` style, `ink-muted`.
- Title in `h1`: ALL CAPS, Poppins Bold, 0.18em tracking, one line where possible.
- One 3pt `bronze` rule under the title. It is the only bronze on the opening block.
- Page margin is `space-12` (0.5in). Keep the logo 0.25in inside trim.
- Opening paragraph follows "story sets up, spec closes": one warm sentence, one hinge, then facts.
- On later pages use the word "Jackman" in `eyebrow` style as a running head, not the logo.
- **Don't** put the lockup on a bronze or yellow band, recolor it, or place it over a photograph.
