Callouts pull one statement out of the page. Provide a short label and one to two sentences.

- A 6px bar on the left carries the meaning; the text is always `ink` on `surface-raised` (10.1:1 on dark, 17:1 on light).
- Bar colors: `bronze` for notes and heritage context, `signal` for safety, torque and do-not warnings, `highlight` for the one point the reader must not miss.
- Use at most one `highlight` callout per page or screen.
- The label is `eyebrow` style. Body is `body`.
- Never set warning text in `signal` on a dark ground (2.3:1). The bar is the signal; the words stay `ink`.
- Corners are `radius-none`. No icons, no shadows.
