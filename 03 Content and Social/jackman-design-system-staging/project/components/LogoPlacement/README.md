Logo placement pairs the right artwork with the right ground. Provide a solid ground and room for clear space; the marks bring no padding.

- Light ground: `-black` artwork. Dark ground: `-white` artwork. The filename names the artwork, not the background.
- Clear space is 1X on every side (cap height of JACKMAN); badge clear space is 25% of its diameter. Nothing enters it.
- Minimums: lockup 140px on screen and 1.0in in print; badge 24px on screen and 0.375in in print. Below that, switch to the badge.
- The mark is only ever pure black or pure white. No bronze, no yellow, no tint, no opacity, no effects.
- Use a solid `surface` or `jackman-black` ground, never a busy spoke photograph.
- The preview ground panels are fixed (#1C1C1C and #F4F1EA) so each file is shown on its correct ground in both themes.
