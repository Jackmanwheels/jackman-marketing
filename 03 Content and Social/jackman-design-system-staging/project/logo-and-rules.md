# Logo and rules

## Which file to use

Four official marks live in the Logos asset group. **`-black` and `-white` name the artwork, not the background.** Black art goes on light grounds, white art goes on dark.

| Need | File |
|---|---|
| Light page, document, white sell sheet | `jackman-lockup-stacked-black` |
| Dark slide, site header, dark social post | `jackman-lockup-stacked-white` |
| Square or round slot on light | `jackman-badge-j-black` |
| Square or round slot on dark | `jackman-badge-j-white` |
| Print, apparel, signage, anything cut or etched | The `.pdf` vector master |
| Screen, email, decks | The `.png` |

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space is 1X on all four sides**, where X is the cap height of the word JACKMAN in the lockup. The files have no built-in padding, so add it every time.
- **Badge clear space is 25% of its diameter.**
- Tighter slots: 0.5X in constrained UI, 2X on trade show, vehicle and co-branded layouts.
- **Nothing enters clear space:** no type, rule, photo edge, QR code or bronze bar.

| Mark | Print | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| Stacked lockup | 1.0in / 25mm wide | 140px wide |
| Badge | 0.375in / 10mm | 24px |

Below the lockup minimum, switch to the badge. Do not shrink the lockup further.

## Never

1. Make a horizontal lockup by setting JACKMAN and WHEELS side by side. None exists yet; use the badge in wide, short slots.
2. Put `-black` art on a dark ground or `-white` art on a light one.
3. Color the mark. No bronze, no yellow, no tint, no opacity below 100%.
4. Stretch, skew, rotate, arc, outline, add shadows, glows or gradients.
5. Retype, trace or rebuild it. The letterforms are custom.
6. Place it over busy spoke photography or on a wheel face in a render.
7. Place a dealer or partner logo beside it without a divider rule.
8. Use a PNG in print.
