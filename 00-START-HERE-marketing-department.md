## What this folder is

The **operating layer** of Jackman marketing. It holds the thinking and the work:
plans, briefs, copy decks, campaign files, test notes, reports, policies.

Marketing is one person. This structure is not a division of labor — it is a division
of *your attention*. Each folder is one kind of work, so that when you come back to
something after three weeks on something else, you land where you left off instead of
starting over.

Full explanation of what each folder does for you, and in what order to fill them, is
in `00 Marketing Command Center / 06 Standards and Conventions /
jackman-marketing-department-operating-guide.md`. Read that one.

## What this folder is NOT

It is **not** the asset library. Finished, approved brand and product assets — logos,
product photography, video masters, packaging art — live in
`Shared Documents / Jackman Wheel Co`.

### The one rule that keeps this from becoming a second junk drawer

> **Work in progress lives here. Finished approved assets live in the asset library.
> Nothing lives in both.**

If you are writing it, planning it, testing it, or reporting on it — it belongs here.
The moment it is approved and final, the deliverable moves to the asset library and
this folder keeps the brief, the plan, and the record.

When in doubt: *am I looking for this to understand the work, or to reuse the file?*
Understand → here. Reuse → asset library.

## Three standing rules

**1. Four levels, maximum.**
`00 Marketing Department / [Department] / [Subfolder] / [Project] / files`.
Sync breaks past roughly 400 characters of full path, and the existing library already
runs eight levels deep in places. Depth is where files go to die.

**2. Filenames follow the convention.** Lowercase, hyphenated, `jackman-` prefixed,
ISO dates, `v1` not `final`. Full rules in `00 Marketing Command Center /
06 Standards and Conventions`. Same convention the brand guidelines govern.

**3. Empty folders are a promise, not a failure.** Most of these sit empty for months
and that is correct. An empty `03 Co-op and MDF Programs` is a reminder the program
doesn't exist yet. Don't delete a folder for being empty — delete it only if the
function genuinely doesn't apply.

## Open items this structure does not solve

- **`Jackman-Logins.xlsx` sits at the root of the Marketing document library.** A
  credentials spreadsheet in a shared library is a real exposure. Password manager,
  not SharePoint. Most urgent item here.
- **Duplicate homes already exist.** `Dealer Assets` and `Dealer Marketing`;
  `Marketing Assets`, `Media`, `Product Assets` and `Vehicle Images`. Until those are
  consolidated, a sell sheet has five plausible locations.
- **Loose files at the library root** — two off-road expo spreadsheets and an expo
  photo. They belong in `08 Events / 01 SEMA and Trade Shows / Off-Road Expo 2026`.
- **The brand guidelines list the source of truth (DAM) as [TBD].** This tree plus
  `Jackman Wheel Co` is now the answer. Write it into the guide and ratify it.
- **Folders are not workflow.** Asana holds what is happening; this tree holds what
  was produced. If both try to be both, both rot.
