# 3. Establish 1968 as the founding date

**Date:** 2026-09-15
**Status:** Proposed — mirrors `D-01` in the decisions inbox. Flips to Accepted when the
brand owner ratifies `jackman-brand-change-proposal-founding-date-v1.md`.

---

## Context

Three founding claims were live in market at the same time. The homepage said
"California-born since the 1960s." The Our Story page placed the hand-built wheels in the
mid-1970s. A revived heritage brand was telling three different origin stories to anyone
who read two pages of its own site.

The response was a hard stop: guardrail #5 and playbook rule 1 both banned any founding
decade or year, with "California-born" as the approved substitute. That was the right
call at the time — three claims are worse than none — but it was a holding pattern, not
a position, and it had been holding for months.

The cost of holding was concentrated in one place. Heritage is the pillar we are most
fluent in, 38% of the current grid against a 10% Instagram target, and it was running
with its central fact redacted. Four things were blocked behind it: the Instagram bio
(`P-02`), the heritage weekly slot (`C-05`), the heritage media pitch (`L-05`), and the
"Established 1968. Rebirthed 2026." positioning line.

## Decision

**Established 1968.** Short form: **Est. 1968.**

Guardrail #5 and playbook rule 1 are superseded — but **not by this document and not by
editing them.** Governance is explicit: one canonical guide, nobody forks it. The change
runs through `01 Brand and Creative / 01 Brand Guidelines and Tokens /
jackman-brand-change-proposal-founding-date-v1.md` and takes effect when the brand owner
applies it. Until then the old guardrail stands and copy still says "California-born."

This ADR records why 1968 and what it leaves unresolved. `D-01` in the decisions inbox
carries the answer and its propagation state. Two different jobs, deliberately.

## Consequences

**What this buys.** The Heritage pillar gets its central fact back, and four blocked
items unblock at once. More than that, it converts heritage from something we gesture at
into something we can state — "Established 1968. Rebirthed 2026." is a positioning line;
"California-born" is an evasion wearing a nice coat.

**What it does not buy, and this is the point of writing it down.** The original risk was
never the inconsistency. It was a skeptic disproving the date in a single click. **A
decision resolves the inconsistency. It does not resolve the disprovability.** As of
today, 1968 is a *stated* claim, not a *sourced* one. We have replaced three unsupported
dates with one unsupported date, which is a real improvement and is not the same thing as
being right.

Closing that gap takes a document — an incorporation record, a period advertisement, a
catalog, a dated photograph — scanned into `01 Brand and Creative`. A sourced heritage
claim is a categorically different asset from a stated one, and the sourced version is
the one that earns press rather than merely surviving it. Not a blocker. Worth doing
before the claim ships wide.

**Two documents still contradict this.** Our Story places the hand-built wheels in the
mid-1970s, which is not necessarily a conflict — a company founded in 1968 can plausibly
be hand-building wheels in 1975 — but the page currently reads as a second origin date.
It needs one sentence establishing 1968 as the founding and the mid-70s as a later
chapter. The homepage's "since the 1960s" is consistent but vaguer than we now need;
tighten it to the year.

**The trap to watch.** Unblocking Heritage is not permission to post more of it. Heritage
is already roughly four times overweight on Instagram, and the strategy is explicit that
it routes us toward vintage audiences rather than the person shopping 6-lug for a Sierra.
The right move is better Heritage on Facebook, where the target is 25%, not more Heritage
on the surface where it costs the most. A newly unblocked pillar is exactly the kind of
thing that quietly eats a content calendar.

**Note on numbering.** ADR-0001 expected the `docs/` convention question to be ADR-0002;
ADR-0002 expected it to be ADR-0003. It has now been deferred twice and becomes ADR-0004
when it is taken up. Neither earlier ADR is edited to match. The sequence records what
was actually decided, in the order it was actually decided, which is the only thing that
makes it trustworthy later.
